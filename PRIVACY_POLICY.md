## 5. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 12-22)

You have the right to:

### **Right of Access (Article 15)**
Request all personal data we hold about you.
- **How:** Email privacy@cineworld.com with "Data Subject Access Request"
- **Timeline:** Within 30 days
- **Cost:** Free

### **Right to Rectification (Article 16)**
Correct inaccurate personal data.
- **How:** Profile settings or contact us
- **Timeline:** Within 30 days

### **Right to Erasure (Article 17) - "Right to Be Forgotten"**
Request deletion of your personal data.
- **What gets deleted:**
  - Name, email, password
  - Watchlist (personal)
  - Reviews (anonymized, content remains)
  - Avatar
  - Following/followers list
- **What remains:** Anonymized movie data (for statistics)
- **How:** Settings → Delete Account
- **Timeline:** Within 30 days

### **Right to Data Portability (Article 20)**
Receive data in machine-readable format.
- **Format:** JSON
- **How:** Settings → Export Data
- **Includes:** Watchlist, reviews, ratings, ratings

### **Right to Object (Article 21)**
Opt-out of marketing emails and analytics.
- **How:** Email unsubscribe link or settings
- **Immediate:** Marketing emails
- **30 days:** Analytics (we'll stop processing)

### **Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)**
Limit how we use your data.
- **How:** Contact privacy@cineworld.com
- **Timeline:** 30 days

## 7. Data Security

### We Protect Your Data With:
- ✅ **HTTPS/TLS** encryption in transit
- ✅ **bcrypt** password hashing (10 rounds)
- ✅ **MongoDB encryption** at rest
- ✅ **HttpOnly cookies** (prevents XSS access)
- ✅ **CORS** and **rate limiting** protection
- ✅ **Helmet.js** security headers
- ✅ **Input validation** (Zod) preventing injection
- ✅ **Automated backups** (daily)

### Security Incidents
If we discover a data breach:
1. Notify affected users within **72 hours** (GDPR requirement)
2. Notify authorities if required
3. Provide:
   - Nature of breach
   - Likely consequences
   - Mitigation measures
