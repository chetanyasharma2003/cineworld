## 🎓 Lessons Learned

1. **Caching is Key** — 65-75% cache hit rate significantly reduces database load
2. **Per-User Rate Limiting** — Better UX than global limits for API-heavy apps
3. **Mobile First** — Design responsive from the start, not as an afterthought
4. **Security Hardening** — Add security in layers (CSRF, rate limiting, input validation)
5. **Analytics Matter** — Tracking user behavior enables smarter recommendations
6. **Social Features Scale** — Users engage more when they can share and collaborate
7. **Monetization Simple** — 3 tiers are enough to capture market segments
